Is the cycle reset in, or still ahead?

Twenty-two scored indicators, each voting bull, bear or neutral against a fixed threshold, in the six groups a cycle analyst would tally: the on-chain resets that preceded every prior low, the cycle clock, price against its own history, market and macro, trend, and the social rows. Bull means the reset is in place or the trend rule is up; bear means the top zone or the trend rule down. The tally leans only when bull votes lead bear votes by four, and the lean follows the page's three-close rule before it can flip. Rows without a free feed, and rows whose level drifts too much across cycles to be thresholded, are listed without a vote rather than dropped.

Feeds: Coin Metrics community API (price, MVRV, market cap, issuance, fees, exchange flows, supply; daily since 2010, one request), DefiLlama (stablecoin supply), CoinGecko (dominance and total cap, logged nightly), Bybit, OKX or Hyperliquid (perpetual funding, whichever answers), alternative.me (fear and greed), Wikimedia (daily pageviews of the Bitcoin article since 2015), the CFTC through section 6, Yahoo (the dollar index), the section 1 liquidity composite. Calibration: at the 2015, 2018 and 2022 lows the Puell multiple read 0.31 to 0.56, MVRV 0.56 to 1.00, the ThermoCap multiple 2 to 7, weekly RSI 28 to 33, monthly RSI 41 to 48, the Mayer multiple 0.40 to 0.71, the drawdown 71% to 85%, the one-year return minus 73% to minus 82%; at the 2021 and 2025 tops Puell 1.1 to 2.5, MVRV 2.3 to 3.4, ThermoCap 29 to 45, weekly RSI 56 to 68, the Mayer multiple 1.18 to 1.47. Tops have come in lower every cycle, so the bear lines sit at the last two tops and will miss a 2013-sized blow-off by a mile; on the Mayer multiple both of the last two tops printed under the bear line, which is why the row says so. The 200-week average is the cycle floor: every cycle has touched or broken it (the minimums read 0.99, 1.00, 0.97 and 0.68) while the tops reached 2.3 to 15.4. Pi Cycle: the bottom fires when the 150-day EMA crosses below 0.745 times the 471-day SMA (14 Jan 2015, 16 Dec 2018, 13 Jul 2022), the top when the 111-day SMA crosses above twice the 350-day SMA (Apr and Dec 2013, Dec 2017, Apr 2021). Shown without a vote: dominance and attention, because both levels drift across cycles (the October 2025 high printed at the 2nd percentile of attention, and the 30-day average has sat in the bottom fifth of its three-year range for most of the past year, so a threshold would be on four fifths of the time). Tested and left off: hash ribbons (16 firings in 15 years, four in 2026 alone: a miner-stress oscillator, not a cycle marker), exchange supply share (in secular decline, and it duplicates the 30-day flow row), the CME basis (the same leverage appetite perpetual funding already measures, plus an expiry guess), CME gaps (narrative, not a rule), app store ranks and whale cohorts (no free feed), spot ETF flows (Farside is behind Cloudflare and the iShares endpoints serve HTML, not JSON), and any regression level shown as a target.
